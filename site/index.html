After a storm

Hail damage isn't always visible from the ground.

If your neighborhood has had a hail or wind event, it's worth a free inspection even if your roof looks fine from the driveway. We'll tell you plainly if there's nothing to claim — we'd rather you trust us next time than push a claim that isn't there.

How the claims process works

What we check in a storm inspection

  • Shingle granule loss and bruising
  • Soft-metal damage (vents, flashing, gutters) as a hail indicator
  • Flashing and sealant integrity around penetrations
  • Attic ventilation and any interior leak evidence
