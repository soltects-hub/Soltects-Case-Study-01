Our approach

A roofing company built around one idea.

Most of what frustrates homeowners about roofing contractors isn't the roofing — it's the uncertainty. A verbal quote that changes once the crew is on the roof. A "free inspection" that turns into a hard sales pitch. We built Fieldstone's process to close those gaps, not paper over them.

Every inspection is genuinely free and comes with a written summary. Every estimate is itemized before you sign anything. Every job is backed by a workmanship warranty, separate from the shingle manufacturer's material warranty.

From first call to final walkthrough

Our process.

01

Initial consultation

A short call or form to understand what's going on with your roof and whether it's a planned project or storm-related.

02

Roof inspection

A project consultant inspects your roof and photographs any damage, usually within 48 hours of your call.

03

Proposal & planning

You get a written scope and price. If it's a storm claim, we prepare documentation for your adjuster.

04

Project execution

Most residential replacements are completed in a single day. We protect landscaping and haul away debris same-day.

05

Final walkthrough

A final walkthrough with photos, your warranty paperwork, and a point of contact if anything needs attention.
