About Fieldstone

A Kansas City–area roofing crew built around a simple idea: tell the homeowner the truth, put it in writing, and show up when you said you would.

How we operate

Most of what frustrates homeowners about roofing contractors isn't the roofing — it's the uncertainty. A verbal quote that changes once the crew is on the roof. A "free inspection" that turns into a hard sales pitch. A claim promise that doesn't match what the insurer actually approves. We built Fieldstone's process to close those gaps, not paper over them.

In practice, that means every inspection is genuinely free and comes with a written summary, every estimate is itemized before you sign anything, and every job is backed by a workmanship warranty that's a separate document from the shingle manufacturer's material warranty — so you know which one to call if something goes wrong and when.

Local crews, not subcontracted labor

Fieldstone's installation crews work directly for Fieldstone. We don't bid your job out to the lowest subcontractor the week of installation, which means the people who inspected your roof are accountable for the people installing it.

Licensing & insurance

Fieldstone operates as a licensed, insured contractor in Kansas and Missouri. If you're evaluating any roofing contractor — including us — ask for a license number and proof of insurance before signing anything, and verify it independently rather than taking a sales rep's word for it.

Why homeowners work with us

  • Free, no-pressure inspection — a written assessment of your roof's condition, no obligation attached.
  • Plain-English estimates — line-item pricing in writing before any work starts.
  • Insurance claim support — we document damage and can meet your adjuster on-site; your insurer still makes the coverage decision.
  • Workmanship warranty — a separate written warranty on our installation, distinct from the manufacturer's material warranty.

Fieldstone Roofing & Exteriors does not exist. This entire website — the brand, the copy, the photography treatment, the forms, and the business described above — was designed and built by Soltects, a digital growth and marketing agency, as the first entry in its own portfolio: a self-initiated case study demonstrating website strategy, UX/CRO, SEO, and front-end development for the US home-services market.

Nothing on this site reflects a real client relationship, a real roofing company's real reviews, certifications, or business history, or real measured business results for "Fieldstone." Where the project's own case study makes performance or testing claims, those are about the demonstration website itself (page speed, accessibility, structure) — not about leads generated or revenue for a real business, because none exists.

If you're a business owner evaluating Soltects, this project is meant to show how we'd approach your site, not to claim we've already run one for a company called Fieldstone.
