Frequently asked questions

Pricing, financing, insurance claims, scheduling, materials, and warranties — in plain language.

General

We're based in Overland Park, KS and run jobs across the Kansas City metro, including Olathe, Lenexa, Shawnee, Kansas City MO, and Independence. If you're near the edge of that area, ask — we'll tell you honestly whether it's a trip we can make.

Roughly: isolated leaks, a handful of missing or cracked shingles, or flashing failures on an otherwise healthy roof usually point to a repair. Widespread granule loss, a roof past its expected material lifespan, or damage spread across multiple slopes usually point to replacement. We'll give you our honest read during the inspection, including when a repair is the right call even if replacement is the bigger job for us.

For active leaks during business hours, call rather than submitting the online form — we prioritize active-leak calls for same-day temporary tarping ahead of scheduled estimates where our crew availability allows it.

Pricing & Financing

It depends on roof size, pitch, material, and current condition, so we don't quote a number sight-unseen. You'll get an itemized written estimate after the free inspection — not a verbal ballpark that changes once we're on the roof.

We can walk you through financing options at the estimate stage. Terms depend on the lender and your credit, so ask your project consultant for current details rather than relying on anything you've seen advertised elsewhere.

Deposit terms are spelled out in your written agreement before any work begins — we don't ask for full payment up front. Review the agreement closely and ask questions before signing.

Storm Damage & Insurance Claims

Yes, if you'd like us there. We point out what our inspection found; the adjuster writes the official scope. See our storm & insurance claims page for the full process.

That's the general idea when a claim is approved at the amount needed to cover the work — your deductible is yours to pay regardless of contractor, and we don't waive or absorb it. But the final number depends entirely on what your insurer approves, so verify specifics with your policy and adjuster rather than taking any contractor's word for it, including ours.

The free inspection and documentation don't cost you anything regardless of outcome. If a claim is denied, you're not obligated to move forward with paid repairs through us, and you're welcome to get a second opinion.

Ask for a local address and a license number you can verify, a written estimate before any deposit, and clarity on who's present for your adjuster meeting. A contractor who's vague on any of those is worth a second call to someone else — see our insurance claims page for more questions worth asking.

Process & Scheduling

Usually 30–45 minutes on the roof and in the attic, plus a short conversation afterward about what we found.

Most single-family homes are a one-day tear-off and install, weather permitting. Steeper, larger, or multi-layer roofs can run two days. We'll give you a specific estimate once we've seen your roof.

We use tarps and plywood to protect landscaping, siding, and windows from falling debris, and we haul away old material and clean up with a magnetic nail sweep the same day. Flag anything especially delicate (irrigation lines, young plantings) when you book so we can plan around it.

Materials & Warranty

Architectural asphalt shingles, standing-seam metal, and synthetic/composite systems. Material options and manufacturer availability vary by project, so we'll go through actual options and pricing at your estimate rather than listing specific brands here.

The material warranty comes from the shingle or metal manufacturer and covers defects in the product itself. The workmanship warranty is ours, and covers issues caused by installation — these are separate documents with separate terms, and we'll give you both in writing.
