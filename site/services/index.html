Materials

Materials we work with

Asphalt shingle

The most common residential roofing material in the metro — a wide range of colors and warranty tiers at a range of price points.

Standing-seam metal

A longer-lived, higher-upfront-cost system that holds up well to hail and sheds snow and water faster than shingle.

Synthetic & composite slate

Lightweight alternatives that mimic the look of slate or shake without the structural weight or maintenance.

Paying for it

Financing
