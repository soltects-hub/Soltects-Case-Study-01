Roof replacement

Full tear-off and replacement, usually completed in a single day for most Kansas City homes.

Signs it's time for a new roof, not another repair

  • Age. Most asphalt shingle roofs in this climate are in their replacement window somewhere between 18 and 25 years, depending on the product and ventilation.
  • Widespread wear. Curling, cracking, or bald (granule-loss) shingles across most of the roof rather than in one isolated spot.
  • Repeated leaks. If the same area has been patched more than once, the underlying deck or underlayment may be the real problem.
  • A failed inspection. Some home sales, insurance renewals, or storm inspections flag a roof as past its useful life even without an active leak.

If your roof doesn't show these signs, a targeted repair is often the more sensible call — we'll tell you which one applies after a free inspection.

Materials

We install asphalt shingle, standing-seam metal, and synthetic/composite systems. Which one makes sense depends on your budget, how long you plan to stay in the home, and your roof's pitch and complexity — this gets covered in detail during your estimate.

What the install day looks like

  1. Tear-off. Old shingles, underlayment, and any damaged flashing are removed down to the deck.
  2. Deck inspection & repair. Any soft or water-damaged decking is identified and replaced before anything new goes down — this is called out separately if it wasn't part of the original estimate.
  3. Underlayment & flashing. New synthetic underlayment, ice-and-water barrier where needed, and flashing around penetrations (vents, chimneys, skylights).
  4. Install. New roofing material installed to manufacturer specification for the warranty you've selected.
  5. Cleanup & haul-away. Magnetic nail sweep of the yard and driveway, debris hauled away same day.
  6. Final walkthrough. We walk the property with you, note anything that needs follow-up, and hand over warranty paperwork.

Questions we hear most

Will you match my HOA's approved colors?

We can work within most HOA architectural guidelines — bring your HOA's approved color/material list to your estimate and we'll confirm availability.

What happens if you find rotten decking once you start?

We stop, show you the damage with photos, and give you a price for the repair before continuing — it's never added to the invoice as a surprise afterward.
