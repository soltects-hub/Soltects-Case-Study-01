Privacy Policy

Effective January 1, 2026

About this document: Fieldstone Roofing & Exteriors is a fictional business created for a Soltects portfolio case study. This page is sample privacy-policy content written to demonstrate what a real contractor's site should include — it is not a real company's legal policy and should not be relied on as one. If you are adapting this structure for an actual business, have it reviewed by a licensed attorney in your state.

What information we collect

We collect information you provide directly through this website's forms, and limited technical information collected automatically when you visit.

Information you provide

  • Name, phone number, and email address (Request a Free Inspection and Contact forms)
  • Property street address and ZIP code (quote request form)
  • Details you choose to share about your project, your roof's condition, or an insurance claim

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, standard server and analytics logs may capture your approximate location (from IP address), browser and device type, pages visited, and referring site. This demonstration site does not currently run third-party analytics or advertising trackers; if a production version of this site added them, this section would name the provider and link to its policy.

How we use this information

  • To respond to inspection and contact requests, including scheduling and follow-up communication by phone, text, or email
  • To prepare written estimates and, where applicable, insurance claim documentation
  • To maintain basic business records of inquiries

We do not sell the personal information submitted through this site to third parties, and we do not use it for unrelated marketing without your consent.

Cookies & similar technologies

This site may use strictly necessary cookies required for basic functionality (for example, remembering that you dismissed a banner). It does not currently set advertising or cross-site tracking cookies. Your browser settings let you block or delete cookies at any time; doing so should not affect your ability to browse the site, though form submission requires JavaScript to be enabled.

How long we keep information

We retain form submissions for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to your request and to maintain records of completed or prospective work, and delete or anonymize records we no longer need for a legitimate business purpose.

Your choices

To ask what information we hold about you, to correct it, or to request deletion, email office@fieldstoneroofingkc.example. We'll respond within a reasonable time.

Children's privacy

This site is intended for homeowners and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our practices change. The effective date above reflects the most recent revision.
